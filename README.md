# Easy Landlord

The website for **Easy Landlord** — a rental app for people who look after one or two flats themselves, and currently keep it all in a notebook, in old text messages, and in photos buried somewhere in WhatsApp.

**https://easylandlord.land**

## What the app does

- Everything about one property on a single screen: tenant, rent, expenses, paperwork
- Tells you **before** the rent is late, not after
- Expenses with a photo of the receipt — the phone reads the amount and the date itself, and you confirm it
- A one-page report you can hand to an accountant
- Photos from before the tenant moves in and after they leave, so the deposit is evidence rather than an argument
- Deposits, lease end dates and contact details tracked in one place
- Ready-made reminder messages, sent through the SMS or WhatsApp app you already use
- Reminds you about the things landlords forget — the move-out photos, the deposit never returned, the lease about to end
- Backup and restore, so changing phone does not lose the record
- Works with no internet
- Ten languages

## What it deliberately does not do

- **No account.** No sign-up, no password, nothing to forget
- **No cloud.** Your tenants' names never leave your phone
- No ads and no tracking

Free for three properties.
