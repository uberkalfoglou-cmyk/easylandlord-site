See who has paid, without thinking

Every property on one screen with a coloured dot. Green: the rent is in. Amber: due. Red: past the grace period you set. Grey: the flat is empty. The ones that need you sit at the top.

No account, no cloud, nothing to sign up for

Your tenants' names stay on your phone. There is no account to create and no server holding your records, so the app works with the internet switched off. The only thing it ever sends is an anonymous report if it crashes, and you can switch even that off. A backup is a file you export and put wherever you like.
